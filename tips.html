Productivity Tips

Simple habits that can make your study sessions better.

🎯

Set One Goal

Choose one important task and finish it before moving on.

⏰

Use Time Blocks

Divide your study time into focused sessions with short breaks.

📵

Remove Distractions

Keep unnecessary notifications and distractions away while studying.

💧

Take Breaks

Short breaks help you refresh your mind and maintain focus.

🌙

Sleep Well

A good night's sleep helps your brain learn and remember better.

✨

Stay Consistent

Small progress every day is better than trying to do everything at once.
